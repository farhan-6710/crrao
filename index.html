Our Legacy

Named after a global icon of statistics.

Professor Calyampudi Radhakrishna Rao — known worldwide as C.R. Rao — is one of the most influential statisticians in modern history. The Cramér-Rao bound, Rao-Blackwell theorem and Rao's score test are taught in every advanced stats classroom in the world.

"All knowledge is, in final analysis, history. All sciences are, in the abstract, mathematics. All judgements are, in their rationale, statistics."

— Prof. C.R. Rao

In 2023, he was awarded the International Prize in Statistics — often described as the Nobel Prize of statistics. This is the legacy our students inherit.
