Eligibility

Who Can Apply for B.Tech 2026-27

Clear, transparent criteria. We evaluate applications on merit and entrance scores.

1

Academic Qualification

Passed (or appearing in 2026) 10+2 / equivalent with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics from a recognized board with minimum 60% aggregate (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL).

2

Entrance Exam

Valid score in TS EAMCET / AP EAMCET / JEE Main 2026. Direct admissions also available through the AIMSCS Institute Aptitude Test for management quota.

3

Age Criteria

Candidates must have been born on or after 1 October 2000. There is no upper age limit for SC/ST candidates.

4

Counselling Mode

Admissions through TGCHE convener counselling for state quota and institute counselling for management quota seats.

FAQ

Admissions Questions, Answered

Couldn't find what you're looking for? Talk to our admissions counsellor on WhatsApp.

Is CR Rao AIMSCS approved by AICTE and JNTUH?

Yes. CR Rao AIMSCS B.Tech is AICTE Approved (EoA 2026-27) and affiliated to JNTUH (Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad). Degrees issued are JNTUH B.Tech degrees.

What entrance exams do you accept?

We accept TS EAMCET, AP EAMCET and JEE Main scores for state and management quota seats. Candidates appearing for any of these exams in 2026 are eligible to apply.

How are seats distributed between specializations?

Each B.Tech specialization has 60 seats — total 300 seats across 5 programs. Convener quota and management quota are split as per Telangana State norms.

Do you provide hostel facilities?

Yes. Separate hostels for boys and girls within the University of Hyderabad campus. Hostel allotment is on a first-come, first-served basis after seat confirmation. Mess charges are billed separately.

What is the total cost of the B.Tech program?

Annual tuition fee is ₹3,00,000. Total 4-year cost (tuition only) is ₹12 Lakh. Hostel & mess are separate. EMI options and education loan tie-ups available with leading banks.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Merit scholarships up to 100% tuition waiver for top JEE / EAMCET ranks. Need-based scholarships, sibling discounts and minority scholarships are also offered. View full scholarship policy →

Can I switch specializations after the first year?

Inter-specialization migration is permitted at the end of the first year, subject to merit and seat availability. The institute follows JNTUH norms for branch change.

What is the placement record?

Our 2024-25 batch achieved 95% placements. Highest CTC was ₹55 LPA from Goldman Sachs. Average tech CTC is ₹12 LPA. See full placement report →
