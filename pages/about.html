Our Story

Founded on a legacy. Built for the future.

CR Rao AIMSCS was founded in 2007 to honour the lifetime contributions of Prof. Calyampudi Radhakrishna Rao — one of the most influential statisticians in modern history. Originally established as a research institute under the Government of India and the State of Andhra Pradesh, it expanded into engineering education with our first B.Tech intake in 2026.

Located inside the prestigious University of Hyderabad campus, AIMSCS gives undergraduates direct access to a research environment few engineering colleges can match — world-class libraries, PhD scholars, distinguished visitors and DST-funded laboratories.

Our mission is simple: produce engineers who can build, who can question and who carry the C.R. Rao legacy of mathematical rigour into the AI economy of the next decade.
