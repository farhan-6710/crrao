FAQ

B.Tech Cyber Security — Common Questions

Do I need prior security knowledge?

No. The first three semesters build CS fundamentals. Specialised security courses begin in Sem 4. We teach from beginner to red-team-ready in 4 years.

Is hacking taught legally?

Absolutely. All offensive labs are run inside an isolated cyber range with vulnerable systems we own. Students learn responsible disclosure and ethics from day one.

Will I get certified?

Yes. The institute sponsors industry certifications including CEH, Security+, AWS Security Specialty and CCNA Security. Many students complete OSCP independently.

Are there CTF competitions?

The AIMSCS CTF Team participates in CSAW, Cyber Security Challenge India, NullCon HackIM and other national / global CTFs throughout the year.
