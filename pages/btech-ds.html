B.Tech Data Science — Common Questions

How is Data Science different from AI & ML?

Data Science emphasises statistical inference, experimentation and end-to-end data pipelines. AI & ML focuses on building and deploying ML/DL models. Data Science graduates often work as data scientists, data engineers and analysts; AI & ML graduates as ML/AI engineers and researchers.

Will I work with real-world data?

Yes. Industry partners (healthcare, fintech, e-commerce) provide anonymised datasets for capstones. Many students publish notebooks, win Kaggle medals and intern with data-driven companies.

Is the programme more statistics-heavy?

Stronger statistics than AI/ML — yes. We cover classical inference, Bayesian analysis, time series and causal inference. The maths foundation here is exceptional thanks to the C.R. Rao legacy.

Can I move into pure ML / AI roles?

Absolutely. Many graduates work as ML engineers at FAANG companies. The programme covers deep learning, MLOps and applied ML in semesters 4–6.
