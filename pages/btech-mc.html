FAQ

B.Tech Math & Computing — Common Questions

How is this different from a CSE degree?

You get all CS fundamentals (DSA, OS, Algorithms, ML) plus deep mathematics — Real Analysis, Stochastic Calculus, Optimization, Numerical Methods. The maths depth is what unlocks quant and research careers.

Will I be ready for quant interviews?

Yes. Sem 5–6 cover mathematical finance, derivatives, time series and stochastic calculus — the core quant interview material. Mock quant interviews and probability puzzle sessions run year-round.

Can I switch to a CS-only career?

Absolutely. The CS coursework matches a CSE degree. Many graduates pursue software engineering, ML or research roles in addition to quant.

Is this programme good for higher studies?

Excellent. The mathematical rigour is highly valued by top global PhD programmes. ~30% of graduates pursue MS/PhD at IISc, IITs and top US universities.
