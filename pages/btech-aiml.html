Program Overview

Engineer the AI systems that will define the next decade.

Our 4-year B.Tech in AI & ML is built around a strong mathematical core, hands-on engineering and live research. Students train large models, deploy production ML pipelines and contribute to faculty-led research from year two.

You won't just take theory courses — you'll spend more than half your credits on labs, projects and research. Our NVIDIA GPU cluster is open to undergrads. Industry mentors from Goldman Sachs, Microsoft and IBM review your capstone.

FAQ

B.Tech AI & ML — Common Questions

Is prior coding experience required?

No. We start from first principles. Students with strong PCM scores from any board can succeed in this program. Semesters 1 and 2 build coding fundamentals from scratch.

How is this different from a CSE degree?

You get all CS fundamentals (DSA, OS, Networks, DBMS) plus dedicated AI/ML coursework — Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, MLOps. The capstone is mandatorily AI/ML focused.

Will I work on real projects?

Yes — two mini-projects, one mandatory industry internship, and one major capstone. Many capstones become research publications or product launches.

What kind of placement support is provided?

Dedicated placement cell, mock interviews, technical training, alumni mentorship, and on-campus visits from 100+ recruiters including Goldman Sachs, Microsoft, Amazon and IBM.

Can I switch to research after B.Tech?

Absolutely. ~25% of our AI & ML graduates pursue MS/PhD at IISc, IITs and top global universities. UoH library access and faculty mentorship strongly support research applications.
