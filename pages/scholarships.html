FAQ

Scholarship — Common Questions

Can I combine scholarships?

Yes — merit and category-based scholarships can stack up to 100% of tuition. Need-based aid is added if applicable. Maximum total cannot exceed 100% of annual tuition.

Is the scholarship for all 4 years?

Yes, subject to maintaining the minimum CGPA stated for that scholarship tier. The institute reviews scholarship continuation annually.

Are international students eligible?

Currently, scholarships are offered to Indian nationals and OCI cardholders. International students can apply for fee concessions on a case-by-case basis.

Can I get an education loan?

Yes. AIMSCS has tie-ups with HDFC, SBI, Axis and ICICI banks for fast-track student loans up to ₹15L. The admissions cell helps you with documentation.
